{%- comment -%} Collects the D/ and T/ pages a page depends on, in order of first appearance, without duplicates or self-links. Two kinds of link are left out: - links in **Remark:**, **Example(s):** and **Intuition:** paragraphs, which are "see also" links rather than dependencies. Such a paragraph starts a see-also stretch; any other bold label (**Proof:**, **Step 1:**, ...) ends it, and unlabeled paragraphs (lists, display math) continue whichever is current; - targets listed in the page's `ignore_edges` front matter, for the rare non-dependency link outside those paragraphs. scripts/check.py applies the same rules; keep the two in step. Usage: {% include out-links.html content=p.content self=p.path ignore=p.ignore_edges %} Result: the array `out_links`, e.g. ["D/set", "T/de-morgan"] Works on both raw markdown ("**Remark:**", "](../D/set)") and rendered HTML ("

Remark:", 'href="../D/set"'), since site.pages may hold either during a build; both separate paragraphs with a blank line. Variables are prefixed _ol_ because includes share the caller's scope. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture _ol_blank_line %} {% endcapture -%} {%- assign out_links = "" | split: "" -%} {%- assign _ol_self = include.self | remove: ".md" -%} {%- assign _ol_aside = false -%} {%- assign _ol_paras = include.content | split: _ol_blank_line -%} {%- for _ol_para in _ol_paras -%} {%- assign _ol_head = _ol_para | strip | slice: 0, 40 | remove_first: "

" | replace_first: "", "**" -%} {%- assign _ol_mark = _ol_head | slice: 0, 2 -%} {%- if _ol_mark == "**" -%} {%- assign _ol_r = _ol_head | slice: 2, 6 -%} {%- assign _ol_e = _ol_head | slice: 2, 7 -%} {%- assign _ol_i = _ol_head | slice: 2, 9 -%} {%- if _ol_r == "Remark" or _ol_e == "Example" or _ol_i == "Intuition" -%} {%- assign _ol_aside = true -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign _ol_aside = false -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {%- unless _ol_aside -%} {%- assign _ol_parts = _ol_para | split: "../" -%} {%- for _ol_part in _ol_parts offset: 1 -%} {%- assign _ol_dir = _ol_part | slice: 0, 2 -%} {%- if _ol_dir == "D/" or _ol_dir == "T/" -%} {%- assign _ol_target = _ol_part | split: ")" | first | split: '"' | first | split: "#" | first | remove: ".md" | remove: ".html" | strip -%} {%- unless _ol_target == _ol_self or include.ignore contains _ol_target -%} {%- assign out_links = out_links | push: _ol_target -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- endunless -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign out_links = out_links | uniq -%}
