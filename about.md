**AnalysisGraph** is a graph of real analysis: an open, rigorous, and readable collection of definitions and theorems with full proofs, in which every page is linked to the results it builds on and the results that build on it. It is intended for undergraduate mathematics students encountering the subject for the first time, and for anyone who wants to see how the pieces of analysis fit together.

The material loosely follows the structure of Stephen Abbott's *Understanding Analysis* (2nd ed., Springer, 2015), covering the real number system, sequences and series, continuity, differentiation, and integration. It aims to present definitions and theorems clearly and concisely, with full proofs.

AnalysisGraph was created as a personal project to deepen my own understanding of real analysis. It is a work in progress and new content is added regularly.
